02

The Catalogue turns scattered studies into one place to search, interpret and retrieve

1

One metadata contract

Experimental design, perturbation, assay, model system, provenance and licence are validated against a shared schema and linked to ontologies.

2

One stable target identity

Ensembl gene IDs connect evidence across gene-level screens, variant measurements and transcriptomic responses.

3

One access layer

The portal and REST API expose searchable results, JSON metadata and complete CSV or Parquet datasets.

04

Next, integration becomes continuous—and reanalysis becomes broader

Curate faster, keep human judgement

AI-assisted extraction will draft structured metadata; curators will resolve ambiguity, validate ontology mappings and approve every release.

Outcome: richer metadata with a shorter path from publication to Catalogue.

Reanalyse more, measure quality

More kits and experimental designs, author-independent quality metrics and modality-appropriate reanalysis beyond the first Perturb-Seq datasets.

Outcome: results that are easier to compare—and easier to trust.

Release predictably, preserve provenance

Periodic, versioned data products will make additions and analytical changes explicit, reproducible and easier to adopt downstream.

Outcome: stable data products for both exploration and production workflows.

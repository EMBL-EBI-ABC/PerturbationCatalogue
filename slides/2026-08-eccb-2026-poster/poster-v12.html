Perturbation Catalogue

An Integrated Resource for Exploring the Impact of Genetic Perturbations

Kirill Tsukanov, Aleksandr Zakirov, Alexey Sokolov, Mallory Freeberg

Perturbation evidence is multiplying faster than it can be organised

A genome-scale Perturb-Seq study can profile more than 2.5 million cells, and pooled screens can test thousands of targets in one experiment. That scale promises the statistical power to distinguish robust biology from study-specific noise—but the evidence is split across repositories, identifiers, assay conventions, reference genomes and incompatible analysis choices. Without a shared account of what was perturbed, in which model, with which readout and under which pipeline, combining studies remains slow, fragile and difficult to reproduce. The Catalogue makes that cross-study power practical: one curated schema, one stable target identity and one access path for comparing results.

# 16: Define and compute optional combined effects

**What to build:** If the scientific grouping is justified, an analyst can compute cross-study combined effects whose contributing assays, assumptions and uncertainty are explicit.

**Blocked by:** 15: Produce normalized assay effects on Slurm.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The product definition records grouping rules, comparability assumptions, eligible methods and the grain of a combined effect before any combined result is marked usable.
- [ ] Comparable assay results produce combined effects with uncertainty, method identity, diagnostics and links to contributing assay results and source observations.
- [ ] Noncomparable or insufficient groups produce explicit QC reasons instead of a misleading combined effect.
- [ ] Combined effects remain distinct from raw scores and per-assay effects and do not enter public projections without a separate publication decision.
